Label print helper

Sends labels from the Label Sheet to the DYMO printer on this computer.

Looking for your printer…

No labels yet. In the Label Sheet, open the Labels menu, choose what to print, and click Print in the sidebar. The labels will appear here. You can leave this tab open between batches.

Can't reach the DYMO software

  1. Make sure DYMO Connect is installed and open, and the printer is plugged in and on.
  2. Open https://127.0.0.1:41951/DYMO/DLS/Printing/StatusConnected in a new tab. If Chrome warns about privacy, click Advanced, then Proceed. It should say true.
  3. If Chrome asked whether this page can access devices on your local network, it needs Allow. If you blocked it by accident, click the icon at the left of the address bar, find Local network access, set it to Allow, and reload this page.
  4. Click Check again above.

Having trouble? Diagnose asks the DYMO software to preview a few test labels (nothing prints) and shows its full answer. Copy the results and send them to whoever maintains the sheet.
